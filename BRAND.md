# ionstudioapps — Brand & Marketing

## Who We Are

ionstudioapps builds focused productivity tools for individuals and teams. We ship software that removes friction from daily work — task management, team coordination, and AI-powered workflows.

**Products:**
- **wheeltodo** — a wheel-spin task manager for web and mobile (iOS + Android)
- **rocky** — an AI team assistant for Slack that connects Notion, Google Calendar, and memory

---

## Brand Identity

### Tone of Voice
- Friendly but sharp
- Direct — we say what things do, not what they feel like
- Slightly playful (the wheel spin is intentional)
- Confident without being corporate

### Values
- Focus over noise
- Tools that get out of your way
- Built for real teams, not enterprise demos

---

## Target Audience

### wheeltodo
- Individuals who feel overwhelmed by traditional to-do lists
- Students and young professionals
- People who use gamification to stay motivated

### rocky
- Small teams (5–15 people) using Slack + Notion
- Startups and indie studios
- Teams that want AI assistance without switching tools
